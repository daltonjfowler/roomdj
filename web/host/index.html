First time here?

RoomDJ plays music from your Spotify Premium account. Spotify asks each teacher to make a free "app" first: about 5 minutes, only once. Made one already? Skip to step 4.

  1. Open the Spotify dashboard Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.
  2. Create an app Click Create app. Any name works. Paste this as the Redirect URI: Tick Web API and Web Playback SDK, then Save.
  3. Add yourself In your app: Settings, then User Management. Add your name and your Spotify email.
  4. It is on your app's Settings page. Never the Client secret.

                Credits

                🎧 Before your first class

                RoomDJ checks every song before it plays: blocked words, explicit tags, and an AI review of the song's themes and lyrics. It catches a lot. It cannot catch everything.

                A song can still slip through: a clean title over rough lyrics, a brand new release, a remix the checker has not heard yet. Always do your own due diligence. You know your students and your school's rules best, so keep an ear on what plays and use Veto or Ban the moment something gets by.

                You are in charge of what plays in your room. RoomDJ is a helper, not a guarantee.
