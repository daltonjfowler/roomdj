# Teacher guide

What RoomDJ does today (2026-10-07) and how to run it in class. Questions or problems: ask
Dalton.

## What it is

**roomdj.com** is a class music queue. Students join on their Chromebooks with today's class
code, search for songs, suggest them and vote 👍 or 👎. Your computer on the projector plays the
songs with the most votes. A filter keeps it school-appropriate, and you have the last word on
everything. There are no student accounts.

## Before class

1. Open **roomdj.com/host/** on the computer that plays the music (the one on the projector and
   speakers).
2. **Teacher key.** The same shared teacher password as uploadmycode, uploadmylaser, uploadmymodel
   and uploadmycut (Dalton has it in `TEACHER-PASSWORD.txt`). A student teacher can have their own
   second key; ask Dalton. Press **Next**.
3. **Who's teaching?** Press your name. Every teacher shares the same key, so this is how RoomDJ
   keeps each teacher's things apart (there is no PIN: it trusts you). This computer remembers who
   picked last, so next time it goes straight to **Teaching as <your name>**; press **Switch** to
   pick someone else (only before a room is open; switching reloads the page).
   - **＋ Add teacher**: a new coworker types their name (up to 32 letters; students see it, so it
     is checked like a note). They start with the usual settings and empty lists.
   - **Rename** fixes a name. **Hide** takes a teacher off the list without deleting anything;
     **Hidden teachers** at the bottom has **Show again**.
   - **Kept for each teacher** (on every computer): your default settings (Save as my defaults), the
     bell schedule and class period, the banned songs, the songs you allowed (Needs a look and
     Allow anyway), and ⭐ Favorites. **Shared by everyone**: what the AI checker already said about
     a song or a nickname, and the song search.
   - **Kept for each teacher on this computer**: your keyboard shortcuts, Big screen display
     choices, background music link and **Music from**. Spotify and Apple Music logins belong to
     the computer, not the teacher.
   - Everything RoomDJ kept from before teacher profiles (settings, bans, allowed songs,
     favorites) belongs to the first teacher on the list (the legacy profile; "Teacher 1" on a new
     install, renamed to the real name). Every other teacher started fresh.
4. **Music from** (under the player) picks what plays:
   - **Song previews (30 seconds)**: no account needed. Always works. A good first try.
   - **Spotify**: full songs. Needs a **Spotify Premium** account, and that account has to be on
     the user list of RoomDJ's Spotify app (ask Dalton to add it). Press **Connect Spotify** and
     log in. The login stays on this computer only.
   - **YouTube**: full songs as YouTube videos that RoomDJ matches to each song. No account needed.
     Ads may play first, and the video has to stay on screen.
   - **Apple Music** shows up here once Dalton has finished setting it up.
5. **Keep the host page open**: your computer runs the class's song searches (Apple's iTunes
   search answers your school's own internet address, not RoomDJ's server). If it is closed or
   asleep, students see "Your teacher's computer is not answering searches right now".
6. Browsers do not play sound until someone clicks, so press **▶ Start music** once the room is
   open.

## Opening today's room

1. **Class code**: keep the suggestion (three words and a number, like
   `golden walrus lantern 42`) or type your own. **🎲 New suggestion** makes another. A short code
   of your own is easier to type, but also easier to guess from outside the class: the suggested
   ones are the safe choice.
2. **Class ends at**: the room closes then. Pick one of your bell times (P1 8:55, P2 10:10 ...),
   a **Custom time**, or **The end of today** (one room for all your classes). RoomDJ picks the
   next bell to ring for you; bells that have passed today are greyed out. With no bells saved it
   fills in one class period from now (**My class period is ... minutes** in Teacher preferences,
   70 to start). **🔔 Edit bell schedule** adds, renames and removes bell times (up to 12, each
   with a short name and the time class ends, in your own time zone; sorted by time). They are
   saved for you (the teacher picked in Who's teaching?), so they follow you to any computer.
   Opening late in the evening, a time after midnight means tomorrow (opening at 10:58 PM, one
   period ends at 12:08 AM tomorrow).
   **Cleanup starts**: filled in 5 minutes before class ends (empty for the end of today). At that
   time the music fades out and stops, suggestions close ("🧹 Cleanup time! Suggestions are closed
   for today."), and Big screen shows **🧹 Cleanup time!** with a countdown to the bell. Leave it
   empty for no cleanup.
   Students see a small **🔔 Bell in 12:34** at the top of their page (**🧹 Cleanup! Bell in
   0:45** during cleanup). It counts down on RoomDJ's clock, so a Chromebook with the wrong time
   still shows the right countdown.
   **Bell offset** (⚙️ Settings → 🕒 Class time): your school's bell rings 3 seconds late? Set
   **+3** (from -120 to +120 seconds; **Save as my default** keeps it). Class end, cleanup and every
   countdown move by that much; the times you type and see stay your bell schedule's times.
3. Press **Open today's room**. The site address and the code show big on the page. Students go
   to roomdj.com on their Chromebooks and type the code once. **📋 Copy join link** gives a link that
   fills in the code, to post in Google Classroom.
4. **📺 Big screen** shows only the address, the code, the song playing and the next five songs, in
   type the back row can read. **⛶ Full screen** (or **F**) goes full screen with Big screen.
   **Esc** leaves Big screen. Settings, **Teacher preferences** sets its text size and whether it
   shows who suggested each song and the album art.
5. **Change code** gives the room a new code mid-day. Students already in stay in; new devices
   need the new code. **🕒 Change times** next to it moves class end (a bell, a custom time or the
   end of today) and cleanup (empty: no cleanup) while the room is open.
6. **Cleanup time**: the music fades out over about 5 seconds and pauses on the song playing (no
   next song, and no background music); students see a cleanup banner and cannot suggest songs,
   but can still vote. **🎵 Keep the music going** ends cleanup: the music comes back, and
   suggestions open again (unless you had locked them yourself). The room still closes when
   class ends.
7. **Closing the window does not close the room.** Reopen **roomdj.com/host/** on the same
   computer and you are back in it (no key needed). Keep it open in class, though: your computer
   runs the song searches.
8. **On another computer** (or a different browser): type the room's class code and press
   **Take back a room I already opened** (or press **Open today's room**, then **Take back my
   room** when it says the code is already open). It needs the teacher key and the code. The
   room, its queue and its students move to this computer, and the old page shows "This room was
   opened on another computer" and stops. Anyone with a teacher key who knows your class code can
   do this, so a student teacher with their own key could take your room too. A room taken back
   stays the teacher's who opened it (their name, bans, allowed songs and favorites).
9. The host header shows **Teaching as <name>** while the room is open. Students see
   "<name>'s class" at the top of their page (only the room's own teacher, never anyone else).

## During class

**Where things are.** The bar at the top has three menus and two buttons:
**📺 Screen ▾** (Big screen, Full screen, Board link), **🏠 Room ▾** (Change code, Change times,
Lock suggestions, Students, Close room), **📋 Lists ▾** (Needs a look, Blocked today, Top 5,
Favorites, Resting songs, Play recap; a pink number on Lists means songs need a look),
**🔎 Suggest a song** and **⚙️ Settings** (tabs: Songs, Credits, Class time, Display & sound,
Shortcuts, Teacher; it opens on the tab you used last). Each song in Up next has your 👍 (and 👎 while 👎 votes are on) and
a **⋯** menu: ⏫ Play next, ▶ Play now, 🚫 Veto, 💬 Veto or ban with a reason, ⛔ Ban. The **⋯**
next to Skip bans the song playing. On a narrow window the bar wraps onto a second row.

- **Start music, Pause, Skip, Volume** work like any player. The song with the highest score
  (👍 minus 👎, or just 👍 while 👎 votes are off) plays next, unless you pushed one with ⏫ (below) or a student jumped one.
- **👀 Needs a look**: songs the AI checker does not know wait here for you (it first tries reading
  the song's lyrics, see "How the filter works"; "📄 Judged from lyrics: still not sure" means it
  read them and still could not decide). **Allow** puts it in
  the queue (and allows it in your future rooms too), **Remove** says no this time, **Ban** says
  no for good. The 📋 Lists button bumps when a new song arrives.
- **Veto** takes a song out of the queue. **Ban** takes it out and blocks it for good, in this
  room and all your future rooms, including other versions of the same song (single, album,
  radio edit). **⋯ → Ban** next to Skip bans the song playing now.
- **Saying why (optional)**: one tap on Veto, Remove or Ban still works (the student reads
  "👩‍🏫 Teacher's choice" or "⛔ Banned by your teacher"). **💬 Veto or ban with a reason** (in a song's ⋯ menu; the
  small **▾** in Needs a look; ⋯ → Ban with a reason for the song playing) opens a
  quick pick: a reason (Explicit lyrics, Drug references, Alcohol or partying, Sexual content,
  Violence or weapons, Self-harm, Hate or slurs, Heavy swearing, Other) and a short note (up to 80
  letters). The student sees the reason and your note. A note with a rude word is not sent (the
  page tells you). A ban keeps its note, so a student who tries that song later reads it too.
- **🚫 Blocked today**: every song turned down in this room today (the last 100), newest first:
  the time, the song, who suggested it, the reason the student saw, and the full details only you
  see (which blocked word, what the AI checker said). **Allow anyway** puts a song the AI checker
  or a blocked word turned down into the queue for that student (no credit charged; it is allowed
  in your future rooms too, like Allow in Needs a look). Explicit and banned songs cannot be
  allowed from here. **Ban** blocks a song for good (for example one you vetoed that should never
  come back). The list shows blocked words and student nicknames, so open it when the class cannot
  see your screen (it is hidden on Big screen). It is deleted with the room.
- **🔎 Suggest a song** (next to Up next, or press **A**): add a song yourself. Search, then
  **➕ Suggest**. Your pick goes through the same song check as a student's (explicit, blocked
  words, bans, the AI checker, Instrumentals only, Max song length, the repeat cooldown, one song
  per artist, played already); a song the checker does not know waits in your own Needs a look,
  where you Allow it. A "no" shows the reason and the full details (the blocked word too), and goes
  in Blocked today marked 👩‍🏫. It shows as **👩‍🏫 your name** on every screen. It costs no credit,
  has no "songs waiting" limit, and works while suggestions are locked or during cleanup. The ⭐
  Favorites window has **➕ Suggest** on every song too.
- **Your vote**: 👍 / 👎 next to each song in Up next (👍 only while 👎 votes are off: you can still
  Veto), and 👍 next to Skip for the song playing. It
  is one vote, like a student's (the same thumb again takes it back; the song playing takes 👍
  only). It never counts toward Top voter, and your picks never make you Top contributor or Crowd
  favorite. Hidden on Big screen and on the board.
- **⏫ Play next / ▶ Play now** (on each song in Up next): **⏫** moves the song to the very top,
  ahead of the votes and of any student's ⚡ Jump, free (credits on or off). Every screen shows
  "⏫ Teacher pick · plays next". Push several and the one you pushed last plays first. "No two in
  a row by the same artist" does not skip over it (you decided), and the class cannot vote it out
  with 👎 (they can still vote; Veto and Ban still work). **⏬** undoes it: the song goes back to its
  place (behind any Jump it had, else by its votes). **▶** plays the song right now: the song
  playing stops (it counts as a Skip) and the chosen one starts. You confirm first when a song is
  playing. Both are hidden on Big screen and on the board, and students never see the buttons.
- **🔓 Suggestions open / 🔒 locked**: lock stops new suggestions. Students can still vote.
- **👥 Students**: who is in the room and who is here right now. A student in back-to-work time
  shows **📚 2:10** (the time left) and a **Let back in** button that ends it now. **Rename** sends a student back
  to pick a different nickname ("Your teacher asked you to pick a different nickname."); they stay
  in the room, keep their songs, and nobody can use that nickname in this room again. **Kick**
  removes a student; that computer cannot join this room again. If they come back on another device or a cleared
  browser, press **Change code** and do not give it to them.
- **⚙️ Settings** (**Use in this room**, or **Save as my default** for every new room):
  - **Songs each student may have waiting** (1 to 10, start at 2).
  - **👎 votes**: **Off** (to start), **On, counts hidden** or **On, counts shown**. Off: students only
    👍, so every song gets its turn. There are no 👎 buttons (yours too; Veto still works), a song's
    score is its 👍, and no song is ever voted out. 👎 students gave before you turned it off are kept
    but do not count; turn 👎 back on and they count again (nothing leaves the queue at that moment).
    Counts hidden: students can 👎 (the limit below applies), but students and the board see only 👍
    counts, never how many 👎 a song has; you still see them, small and grey (not on Big screen).
    The order still uses the real score. Counts shown: everyone sees 👍, 👎 and the score. A room that
    was already open when this setting arrived keeps 👎 shown until you change it.
  - **Scores never go below 0** (on to start; a room that was already open keeps what it had).
    Votes never remove a song, so every student's song gets to play. 👎 can push a song down. Only
    you can veto. While it is on, every score shows as 0 or more (the queue, the board, Top 5 and the
    recap; the 👍 and 👎 counts stay real), the remove score below is greyed out and removes nothing,
    and songs tied on a score play in this order: fewer 👎 first, then more 👍, then the one suggested
    first. Your ⏫ Play next songs and students' ⚡ jumps still come first, and the 👎 limit still
    counts. Turning it off removes nothing at that moment: from the next vote, any song at or below
    the remove score leaves the queue as usual.
  - **Remove a song when its score falls to** (−1 to −50, start at −3).
  - **👎 per student per class** (1, 2, 3, 5 or Unlimited; start at 3; shown only while 👎 votes are
    on). Stops a few students from
    voting good songs out. Each 👎 a student gives uses one for this room (the class period). If they
    take it back, or change it to 👍, while the song is still waiting, they get it back. Once the song
    plays, is vetoed or leaves the queue, that 👎 stays used. With none left, the page says "You've
    used your 👎 for this class. You can still 👍 songs you like." 👍 is never limited, and your own
    votes never count. A change works at once: lowering it never removes a 👎 already given, it only
    stops new ones. Students → each student shows "👎 2/3" once they have used one.
  - **👻 Ghost 👍** (off to start) and **Give a waiting song +1 after** (1 to 10 minutes, start at
    3). Gives a waiting song a quiet +1 👍 after that many minutes so every song gets some love.
    Students can't tell it apart: on their page and the board it is simply one more 👍 (no ghost
    sign anywhere, not even the setting). Each song gets ONE, counted from when it joined Up next (a
    song you Allow from Needs a look counts from your Allow); songs in Needs a look and the song
    playing never get one. It moves songs up like a real 👍 (your ⏫ Play next and ⚡ jumps still come
    first), so the order can change on its own when a song reaches its minute. On your normal view a
    small grey 👻 next to a song's score tells you its 👍 include the ghost (never on Big screen). A
    song keeps its ghost when it starts playing, so the Top 5 and the recap show the 👍 the class
    saw. It never counts for ⭐ Class favorites, Crowd favorite, Top voter or Top contributor, or the
    👎 limit. Turning it on gives every song that has already waited long enough its ghost at once;
    turning it off takes the ghosts off the waiting songs at once (they come back if you turn it on
    again). With "Scores never go below 0" off and 👎 on, a ghost also counts toward the remove score.
  - **Check every suggestion with the AI song checker** (on to start). It checks new nicknames
    too. Off: only the explicit flag, your blocked words and your bans are checked, and nicknames
    only against the word lists.
  - **Songs the AI checker does not know: ask me first** (on to start). On: they wait in Needs a
    look. Off: they go straight into the queue.
  - **Blocked words**: one per line. Songs and nicknames with these words are blocked, and
    matching songs already waiting leave.
  - **Max song length** (on to start, 5 minutes; 1 to 20): longer songs are turned down with "⏱
    Too long: This song is 7:12; the limit is 5:00." before they cost a credit (students see it on
    their search results too). Off: any length.
  - **Instrumentals only** (off to start), for focus time: songs with singing or rapping are turned
    down with "🎹 Instrumentals only right now: This song has singing." (in Blocked today, where
    **Allow anyway** lets one in). Words like "Instrumental", "Karaoke" or "lofi beats" in the title
    count as instrumental; otherwise the AI checker says (the same check, no extra cost). Songs it is
    not sure about wait in Needs a look (while "ask me first" is on). Classical, jazz and soundtrack
    songs count as instrumental when the checker is not sure, so a sung jazz or film song can slip
    through: Skip or Veto it.
  - **😴 Repeat cooldown** (1 day to start; Off, 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks): a song that played
    in any of your rooms (it finished, or played a minute or more before a skip) rests this long.
    Students see "😴 Resting until Thu" on their search results and Class favorites, and a
    suggestion gets "😴 Resting: This song played on Monday. It can come back on Thursday." It
    costs no credit. Other versions count as the same song (see Repeats below). Off: songs can come
    back in the next class (RoomDJ still keeps the list, so turning it back on works at once).
  - **🔔 Bell schedule…** opens the bell schedule editor (see Opening today's room).
  - **⭐ Class favorites for students** (on to start): students see the songs your classes loved
    (see Top 5, the recap and Class favorites below).
  - **🎨 No back-to-back artist** (off to start): the next song is never by the artist who just
    played (the queue shows the song that really plays next); when every waiting song is by that
    artist, it plays anyway. "Artist" is the main one: "Drake feat. Rihanna" counts as Drake.
  - **🎨 One song per artist** (off to start): a suggestion is turned down with "🎨 One song per
    artist: A song by Drake is already waiting." while a song by that artist waits in the queue or
    in Needs a look. It costs no credit. Your Allow and Allow anyway still let a song in.
  - **Credits** (off to start): an On/Off switch. On shows **Starting credits** (0 to 50; 3 when
    you first switch it on). See Credits below.
  - **Priority jump** (off to start): an On/Off switch for **⚡ Jump**. It works only while credits
    are on (greyed out otherwise). See Credits below.
  - **Save as my default** keeps both switches too, for every new room.
  - **📚 Back to work** (3 minutes to start; Off, 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 minutes): after a student
    suggests a song (it joins the queue or waits in Needs a look), their screen turns into
    "📚 Back to work! Your pick is in. Check back in 3 minutes." with a countdown; search, the
    queue and the votes come back when it ends. Only a suggestion starts it: voting (👍 or 👎) and
    ⚡ Jump never do. While it runs RoomDJ refuses that student's next suggestion and jumps, so a
    reload or another tab does not skip it, and nothing is charged for a refused try. **👥 Students** shows who is
    waiting; **Let back in** ends it for one student. Off lets everyone back in at once. Your own
    picks and votes never wait.
  - **🎵 Recap music and sounds** (on to start) and **Recap volume** (50% of the room's volume to
    start): see ▶ Play recap below.
  - **Teacher preferences**: **My class period is ... minutes** (10 to 240, start at 70; used
    for the class end time when you open a room; **Save as my default** keeps it). **On this
    computer** (saved right away, only on this computer): Big screen text (Smaller, Normal,
    Larger), show who suggested each song, show album art, start in Big screen, and a link to the
    keyboard shortcuts.
  - **Save as my default** saves every setting, including the ones on other parts of the page.
- **Close room** ends it now. Otherwise it closes by itself at its end time, or after 12 hours
  with nothing happening.

## Two computers: speakers + projector

Use this when the computer with the good speakers is not the one on the projector (for example
your desktop has the speakers and your laptop drives the projector). The desktop runs the room
and plays the music; the laptop shows the **board**: the class code, now playing and the queue,
with a small remote for when you stand at the front. **Music never plays on the board.**

**The easy way: sign the laptop in once (a bookmark, no link to send).**

1. On the **laptop** (projector), open **roomdj.com/board** and bookmark it.
2. Type the teacher key, tick **Remember on this computer** (only on a school computer students do
   not use), press **Next** and pick **your name**.
3. That's it. With no room open, the board shows a calm full-screen clock with "Waiting for
   *your name* to open a room…". When you open a room on the **desktop** (speakers), the board
   finds it by itself within about 10 seconds. When the room closes (you close it, or class ends),
   the board goes back to waiting and picks up your next room, every period, all day. Press
   **⛶ Full screen** (or **F**) once; the laptop's screen stays awake.
4. On the desktop, press **▶ Start music** as usual (browsers need one click on the computer that
   plays the sound). The desktop shows **📺 Board connected**.

The board only ever shows **your** rooms, never another teacher's. **👤 Switch teacher** (on the
waiting screen, or the remote's **⋯** menu) follows someone else; **Forget this computer** takes
the teacher key off the laptop. If the teacher password changes, the board asks for the new one.
Without **Remember** ticked, the key stays only until the browser is closed.

**The other way: a link for one room.** On the desktop, press **📺 Board link** in the room's
toolbar, then **📺 Make a board link** and **📋 Copy**, get the link to the laptop (email it to
yourself, or paste it in a doc only you can open) and open it there. A new room needs a new link.

Keep a board link to yourself and **never show it on the projector**: anyone with it can skip,
veto and pause. The board takes it out of its own address bar as soon as it opens, and the link
stops working when the room closes. To cut off an old link (you showed it by mistake, or a
computer you no longer trust has it), press **📺 Board link → 🔁 New board link** on the desktop:
every board on the old link stops at once (it says "A new board link was made"); open the new one
on the laptop. Boards that signed in keep working.

**The remote** sits at the bottom of the board and hides after a few seconds; move the mouse or
press any key to bring it back:

- **⏯ Play / Pause** (Space): pauses or plays the music on the desktop. If the desktop has not had
  its **▶ Start music** click yet, the board says "Start the music on the teacher computer first"
  and the desktop says "Click Start music on this computer".
- **⏭ Skip** (S), **🚫 Veto** (V; asks first): veto stops the song playing now, tells its student,
  keeps it out of the Top 5 and puts it in **🚫 Blocked today** on the desktop. To **ban** a song,
  use the desktop.
- **🔒 Lock suggestions / 🔓 Unlock** (L).
- **▶ Play recap** (R): the awards show plays on the board (← → move between slides, Space pauses,
  Esc closes). **▶ Play recap** on the desktop plays it on the board too (and on the desktop only
  while it is in Big screen).
- **🔉 🔊** (− and +): the desktop's volume.

If the desktop is closed or loses its connection, the board says "The teacher computer is not
connected" and Play / Pause and volume turn off until it is back. Everything else (Needs a look,
settings, students, bans, credits, Blocked today) stays on the desktop: the board shows only how
many songs **👀 Need a look**, never which ones. Its text size, "who suggested" and album art
follow the desktop's **Teacher preferences**. One computer for everything? **📺 Big screen** on
the desktop still works as before. At most 3 boards can be open at once (a fourth stops the
oldest).

## Keyboard shortcuts

On the host page, press **?** (or the **⌨ Press ? for shortcuts** link under the player) to see
them. They do nothing while you type in a box, while another window (Settings, Students ...) is
open, or with Ctrl or Alt held. A screen reader hears what each one did ("Skipped", "Suggestions
locked").

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Space | Play / pause (with nothing from the queue: background music) |
| N | Skip the song |
| B | Big screen on / off |
| F | Full screen on / off (with Big screen) |
| L | Lock / unlock suggestions |
| M | Mute / unmute |
| ↑ / ↓ | Volume up / down |
| G | Background music play / pause |
| A | Suggest a song (your own pick) |
| S | Settings |
| E | Edit layout on / off |
| ? | The shortcuts |

**Your own keys**: in the shortcuts window press **Change** next to one, then the new key. A key
another shortcut has is refused (it says which one). **Reset to the usual keys** puts them back.
Your keys are kept on this computer only.

## Arrange the screen

**📺 Screen → ✏️ Edit layout** (or **E**) lets you arrange the room screen: make a panel's text
bigger for the back row, give Now playing more room, put Up next on the left, or hide a panel the
class does not need. Each panel gets a dashed outline and its own small tools:

- **⠿ Move**: drag a panel by its ⠿ handle onto another column and the two swap places. Or use
  **←** and **→** on the panel.
- **Resize**: drag the bar between two columns to give one more of the width. A focused bar also
  moves with ← and →. No column gets narrower than readable.
- **Text size**: drag the **⤡** corner (down and right for bigger), use **A−** / **A+**, hold Ctrl
  and turn the mouse wheel over the panel, or focus the corner and press the arrow keys or + / −.
  From 60% to 250%. Album art, the class times and buttons grow with the text; bigger text wraps
  (or scrolls) inside its own panel.
- **👁 Hide**: the panel goes; **Hidden: … (show)** in the edit bar brings it back. On your normal
  teacher view, Now playing (Start music, Skip) and Needs a look can never be hidden; on Big
  screen and the board anything can, except the last panel on screen.
- **↺** puts one panel back to its usual size (and shows it); **↺ Reset all** puts everything back.

**Done ✓** or **Esc** finishes. Outside edit mode nothing moves, however the mouse or a touch
screen is used, so nothing gets dragged by accident during class.

Your normal view and Big screen each keep their own layout, kept **on this computer, for each
teacher** (another teacher on the same computer has their own). The board has its own too: on
the board, press **E** or **⋯ → ✏️ Edit layout** in its remote; it is kept on the projector
computer. On a small window (under about 1,100 pixels wide) the panels stack as usual; text sizes
and hidden panels still apply.

## Background music

Music for the quiet moments, while no student song plays. Under the player press **Background
music link** and paste:

- a **YouTube** video, live stream or playlist link (a lofi or jazz stream works well). It plays
  in its own small YouTube player that stays on screen while it plays (YouTube's rule; ads may
  play). No setup needed.
- a **Spotify** playlist or album link (not a single song). It needs **Music from: Spotify**,
  connected, and plays on the same RoomDJ speaker.

**▶ Background music** (or **G**) plays it; press again to pause. A student's song pauses it, and
with **Resume background music when the queue is empty** ticked (it starts ticked) it comes back
by itself when the queue runs out (Spotify picks up where it was). It never plays at the same time
as a student's song. Pressing it while a student song plays pauses that song. Cleanup time and
closing the room stop it. The link is kept on this computer.

## Top 5, the recap and Class favorites

- **🏆 Top 5** (top bar): the five songs that played with the best score this period (👍 minus 👎
  when they ended, counting the 👍 students gave while it played; ties go to more 👍, then the earlier song), 👑 on #1. Skipped songs
  count; a song that would not play, or that you banned while it played, does not. Students see a
  small "Top 5 so far" card. In cleanup time the Top 5 shows under the cleanup banner, on Big
  screen too.
- **▶ Play recap** (in the Top 5 window and under the cleanup banner): a full-screen awards show
  for the projector, like the end of Mario Party: "🏆 Period recap!", then 3 or 4 fun awards picked
  at random (genre mix, longest and shortest song, oldest and newest song, decades, the vibe
  changer, most 👍, the song that survived the most 👎 and the closest vote (those two, and any 👎
  number in the recap, only while 👎 counts are shown), the crowd favorite, music
  time, your class favorite of all time; only ones the period really has), the Top 5 countdown
  from #5 to 👑 #1, and the finale: **🎤 Top contributor** (most songs suggested) **vs 🗳 Top
  voter** (most votes cast). It moves on by itself every few seconds; ← and → (or a click) move
  back and forward, Space pauses, Esc closes. On computers set to reduce motion it fades instead
  of bouncing, with no confetti.
  **🎵 Recap music**: while the show plays (here or on the board), this computer pauses the room
  music and the background music, fades in the period's #1 song (its 30-second preview; when it
  has none, the next Top 5 song that does; none: just the sounds), keeps it going for the whole
  show, and plays a short "ta-da" on each award and a bigger one on 👑 #1. At the end (or Esc) the
  music fades out and the room music comes back exactly as it was (paused stays paused). The
  board never makes a sound: when the show plays there, this computer plays the music and follows
  its slides. Turn it off in Settings (then the room music only turns down while the show plays);
  **Recap volume** sets how loud it is.
- **🎬 End-of-class recap** (in the Top 5 window) shows the same as a card: totals, the versus
  card, the stats (**🎲 More stats** shows others), the Top 5 and every song in order. **📋 Copy
  recap** copies it as text for Google Classroom or an email. When the room closes, the recap
  stays on that page's closed screen (**🎬 Class recap**) until you open a new room; it is never
  saved.
- **⭐ Favorites** (top bar): songs your classes loved, kept from day to day: a song that finishes
  with +2 or better gets in, and when the room closes each song in that period's Top 5 (with a
  score above 0) counts once more. Recent favorites rank higher than old ones. At most 200 songs.
  **📌 Pin** keeps a song at the top; **Remove** takes it off (it can come back if a class votes
  it up again); a ban takes it off too. Only songs are kept, never names. Students see the top 20
  under **⭐ Class favorites** next to the search box and can suggest one with a tap: it goes
  through the same checks as any suggestion (and costs a credit when credits are on). Songs with
  your blocked words or bans never show there; songs that cannot be suggested right now (too long,
  already in the queue, played already, resting) say so.

## Repeats

- **One song, any version**: "Blinding Lights (Remix)", "Heat Waves - Sped Up", "Shake It Off
  (Taylor's Version)", a live, acoustic, clean, remastered or "feat." version all count as the song
  itself. A song never plays twice in one class: another version of a song that played is "Played
  already", and another version of a song waiting in the queue or in Needs a look is "In the
  queue". The title must match as a whole and so must the main artist, so "Happy" and "Happy Now",
  "Yellow" and "Yellow Submarine", or Adele's "Hello" and Lionel Richie's stay different songs.
- **😴 Repeat cooldown** (Settings, Songs; 1 day to start): a song rests across ALL your rooms for
  the time you pick. **⭐ Favorites** shows **😴 Resting songs** (when each played and until when
  it rests); **⏰ Wake now** lets students pick one again straight away. RoomDJ keeps only the song
  (title and artist) and when it last played, for two weeks, never names or devices.

## Credits

Credits are off until you turn the **Credits** switch on in Settings and pick **Starting
credits**. You can turn them on or off any time.

- **Suggesting a song costs 1 credit.** Voting is always free.
- **⚡ Jump costs 2 credits**, and only while **Priority jump** is on too (it is off to start, so
  turning credits on alone does not let anyone jump). It moves any waiting song to the front, so
  it plays next. If several songs are jumped, they play in the order they were jumped, and votes
  order everything after them. The queue shows "⚡ Jumped by" and the student's nickname. A song
  can be jumped once. With Priority jump off, students see no Jump buttons, and a jump costs
  nothing ("Your teacher turned off jumping."). Turning it off during class does not un-jump
  songs already jumped: they keep their place and their "⚡ Jumped by" tag.
- **Credits come back** when the filter says no (explicit, a blocked word, a banned song, not
  right for school) and when you press **Remove** in Needs a look. While a song waits in Needs a
  look its credit is held: **Allow** keeps it spent, **Remove** gives it back, **Ban** does not.
  A song you veto or ban, or the class votes out, does not give its credit back. A suggestion
  refused for another reason (too many waiting, locked, already in the queue) costs nothing.
- **Giving credits**: in **👥 Students**, each student has **+1**, **+5** and **More…** (give any
  number, or **Set to** a number). **Give everyone** gives every student in the room the same
  number. Students see a message when they get credits.
- **Changing Starting credits** during class is for students who join later; students already in
  the room keep what they have (use **Give everyone** for them). Switching credits on from off
  gives everyone in the room the starting amount.
- Credits belong to the computer a student joined on and last for the room (the day). A student
  who rejoins on the same computer keeps them; **Kick** removes them. They are deleted with the room.

## What students see

A search box (they can look for songs that pass a quick check even before joining; each song is
checked again when they suggest it), then the class code and a
nickname. In the room: the song playing, the queue with 👍 buttons (👎 too while you have 👎 votes
on, with the 👎 count only when counts are shown, and a small "👎 2 left" while 👎 per student is
limited), and their own songs marked. They can have only a few songs waiting at once. With credits on, they also see their
credits and what a song costs; with Priority jump on as well, what a jump costs and a **⚡ Jump**
button on each waiting song. When a song is refused they see
the reason and a short explanation, for example "🔞 Explicit lyrics: Apple marks this song
explicit.", "⏱ Too long: This song is 7:12; the limit is 5:00.", "💊 Drug references: References to cocaine use" (the AI checker's own words, never
lyrics, and only when they pass the word list), "📝 Blocked word: It has a word your teacher
blocked." (never the word), "⛔ Banned by your teacher" (with your ban note, if any), or "❓ The
checker does not know this song: Your teacher will check it." A refused song in their search
results says "Not allowed" (or "⏱ Too long") with a **Why?** button. At cleanup time they see
"🧹 Cleanup time! Suggestions are closed for today."; with Instrumentals only on, a note says so. When you veto, remove or ban their song they
see your reason and note. They never see your blocked words, the Needs a look list, Blocked
today, other students' devices or anything about your music account. After they suggest a song
(with 📚 Back to work on; voting never starts it), the page shows "📚 Back to work! Your pick is in. Check back in
3 minutes." with a countdown, and search, the queue and voting come back when it ends (or when you
press Let back in). A
nickname that is rude, that poses as staff ("teacher", "admin", "Mr Smith"), or that someone in
the room already has, is refused with "Please pick a different nickname." (it never says why).

## The filter, and its limits

Every suggestion is checked on the server, never on the student's computer. A song marked
explicit by Apple is refused (or swapped for its clean version, when there is one). Then your
blocked words, then your bans, then the AI checker, which judges what the song is about (sex,
drugs, drinking, violence, self-harm, hate, heavy swearing), even in a clean version. **Clean edits
vs the filter**: a "Clean edit" only bleeps out swear words; the song is still about the same thing,
so the AI checker can still turn it down, and students are told exactly that. Spotify and
Apple Music never play an explicit track. **The AI can be wrong**, both ways, and song details
come from Apple's catalog, which anyone can publish to.

**New songs and lyrics.** The AI model's knowledge stops in 2023, so it does not know most newer
songs. For those it looks the song up on LRCLIB (a free public lyrics library) and reads the lyrics
once to decide. Fewer songs wait in Needs a look this way. Blocked today says "judged from
lyrics" when that is how it decided; students see the usual reason, never the lyrics. The lyrics
are never kept, logged or shown. It reads them strictly, like the rest of the check: in our test a
few plain love songs that mention kissing, touching or undressing were turned down as "sexual
content", so **Allow anyway** in Blocked today is the fix when you disagree (it lets that song in
from then on). If LRCLIB is down, slow, or has no lyrics, the song waits in Needs a look as before.
Spotify songs are never looked up (Spotify's rules: no AI). YouTube has no explicit flag, so a clean
song can still come up as its explicit video. You are the backstop: listen, and Skip, Veto or Ban
anything that slips through. One Ban covers it for good.

**Album covers.** A song's cover can be a problem even when the song is fine (a suggestive photo,
for example). When a song is suggested, an AI model looks at its cover once. A cover that is not
right for class shows as a plain 🎵 tile on every screen; **the song itself is never blocked for
its cover**. A cover that has not been checked yet (for example while the checker is busy) is 🎵
too, and in search results it shows blurred under a 🎵 (searches are not checked, to keep the
cost down). On your page a hidden cover says **🖼 cover hidden** and why; **Show anyway** shows it
from then on, for you. Settings → Display & sound → **Album art**: **🖼 Check album covers** (on to
start; off shows every cover without a check, as before) and **🎵 Hide all album covers** (no covers
at all). **Save as my default** keeps both. RoomDJ only controls its own pictures: Spotify,
YouTube (the video shows, by YouTube's rules) and Apple Music may show their own artwork in their
players. The checker can be wrong, both ways: in our test of 26 well-known covers it hid all 10
problem covers and 1 of 16 fine ones (a pixelated cover). Only the answer is kept, never the
image.

Nicknames go through a built-in word list first (rude, sexual, hateful, drug and violent words,
also when dressed up as `sh1t`, `f.u.c.k` or `fuuuck`; names that pose as staff), then your
blocked words, then, while the AI checker is on, the same AI model, which also catches joke names
like "Mike Hawk". If the AI is down, a nickname the word lists passed is let in (the AI warning
shows), so a class is never stuck outside. The AI can be wrong here too: **Rename** fixes it.

## Privacy

Students type only a nickname: no names, emails or accounts. A room holds nicknames, a random id
for each device, votes and songs (and its Top 5 and recap), and all of it is deleted when the room
closes. Album cover checks keep only the answer for each cover (never the image). **⭐ Favorites** keeps only song details (title, artist, album, cover, length, genre),
never nicknames or devices. Teacher names (Who's teaching?) are kept on RoomDJ's server, and a
room shows its teacher's name to the students in it. The repeat cooldown keeps only each song's
title, artist and when it last played (two weeks at most). Your Spotify or
Apple login stays in your browser and never reaches RoomDJ's server or the students. More on
the **Privacy and legal** page (link at the bottom of every page).

## Troubleshooting

- **No sound**: press **▶ Start music** (browsers need a click). Check the volume slider and the
  computer's speakers. With a board (two computers), the sound comes from the desktop, never the
  board.
- **The board says "This board link does not work"**: a new board link was made, or the room is a
  new one. On the desktop, open **📺 Board link** and open that link on the laptop.
- **Spotify will not play**: it needs Premium, and the account has to be on the app's user list
  (Dalton adds it). "Spotify moved to another device" means a phone or the Spotify app took the
  music: press **Start music** to bring it back.
- **YouTube**: an ad may play first; let it. When YouTube's search limit for the day is used up,
  songs play as 30-second previews until tomorrow.
- **A song is skipped with a notice**: that service does not have it, or has only the explicit
  version.
- **"Your teacher's computer is not answering searches"**: the host page is closed, asleep or
  offline. Open roomdj.com/host/ again on the same computer (the room stays open and it rejoins
  by itself); on another computer use **Take back a room I already opened**. Searches already
  made still work.
- **"A room with that code is already open. Is it yours?"**: you opened it earlier (another
  window or computer). Press **Take back my room**. If it is not yours, pick a different code.
- **"The song search is busy"**: Apple limits how often one school may search (about 20 new
  searches a minute; repeated searches come from RoomDJ's memory). Wait a moment and try again.
- **roomdj.com does not load** (the name is new, and some school networks are slow to learn it):
  wait a few minutes, or restart the computer. On Windows, `ipconfig /flushdns` in a command prompt
  also works.
- **"Pick who is teaching first"**: that teacher was hidden on another computer. Pick a name
  again (or **Show again** under Hidden teachers).
- **"Too many wrong tries"**: a student mistyped the code five times. That one computer waits a
  few seconds; nobody else is locked out.
- **Anything else**: tell Dalton what you clicked and what it said.
